Privacy policy

What personal information we collect when you use FightHub and the Fight Hub training app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and your rights.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. Who is responsible for your information

FightHub is a trading name of Pinnqle Ltd, a company registered in Scotland (company number SC898926), Office 1751, 3 Fitzroy Place, 1/1 Sauchiehall Street, Glasgow, G3 7RH, United Kingdom. We are the controller of your personal information under UK data protection law. For anything about your information, email support@fighthub.world.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and sign-in details. If you sign in with Google or Apple, they share your name, email address and profile picture with us.
  • Your profile: display name, gender, coach voice, main discipline, goal and experience level.
  • Your training journal: the sessions you log (date, type, discipline, minutes, rounds and effort) and any notes you write. Please don’t put detailed medical information in your notes. If you mention something like an injury, we only store it so you can see it, and share it with the voice coach during conversations you start.
  • Training kept on your phone: your routine, training history, weekly goal, achievements and settings are saved in the app on your device, so it works offline. They are not sent to us.
  • Premium and payments: Stripe collects your payment details. We receive your Stripe customer reference, what you bought, and the status and dates of your Premium. We never receive your full card number.
  • Voice coach conversations: see section 4.
  • Messages you send us: if you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider handles technical details such as your IP address and browser type when you visit, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

3. Why we use it, and our legal reasons

  • To create and run your account, sync your journal, provide Premium and the voice coach: to carry out our contract with you.
  • To take payments and keep financial records: to carry out our contract, and because tax law requires it.
  • To keep Fight Hub secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and fix problems: our legitimate interests in running a safe, reliable service.
  • To reply to you and handle complaints, and to send important service messages (for example about your subscription or changes to our terms): our contract with you and our legitimate interests.

We don’t send marketing emails unless you have agreed to receive them, and we don’t use your information for advertising.

4. The AI voice coach

The voice coach only runs when you start a conversation, and your microphone is only used during that conversation. When you talk to it:

  • Your voice is streamed to ElevenLabs, which turns your speech into text and creates the coach’s voice.
  • To make the coach personal, we give it your name, main discipline, goal, experience level, a summary of this week’s training, your five most recent journal entries (including notes) and this month’s challenge.
  • The coach’s replies are written by an AI model reached through OpenRouter; the model we currently use is Google’s Gemini. They receive the text of the conversation and the details above, not your voice.
  • ElevenLabs keeps each conversation (audio and transcript) in our account for up to 30 days, then deletes it. We only look at conversations to check quality, investigate problems or handle complaints.

5. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who handle personal information for us, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Clerk: sign-in and account security.
  • Supabase: the database that stores your profile and journal.
  • Stripe: payments. Stripe also uses some information for its own purposes, such as preventing fraud and meeting legal duties, under its own privacy policy.
  • ElevenLabs, OpenRouter and Google: the voice coach (section 4).
  • Vercel: hosting for the website and the app.
  • Zoho: our email.

We don’t sell your personal information. We may share it if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or if our business (or FightHub) is transferred to another company, which would then have to protect it in the same way.

6. Information sent outside the UK

Some of these providers are based in, or access information from, the USA and other countries. When personal information leaves the UK, it is protected by UK adequacy regulations (for example, the UK extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified US companies) or by the UK’s approved data transfer contracts.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and journal: while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile and journal are deleted from our database straight away and your sign-in is deleted. Any backup copies are deleted when those backups expire.
  • Payment and Premium records: kept by us and Stripe for as long as the law requires, usually six years, for tax and accounting.
  • Voice coach conversations: up to 30 days (section 4).
  • Emails with us: up to two years after our last contact, unless we need them longer for a complaint or legal claim.
  • Training kept on your phone: until you clear the app’s data in your browser or remove the app.

8. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. You can change your profile and delete your account yourself in the app. For anything else, email support@fighthub.world. We’ll reply within one month, and there is normally no charge.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113.

9. Cookies and storage on your device

  • Signing in uses essential cookies from Clerk to keep you signed in securely.
  • The app saves your training and settings in your browser’s storage, and keeps its files on your phone so it works offline. This is essential for the app to work.
  • Stripe uses cookies on its secure checkout pages to take payments and prevent fraud.
  • The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts and some pictures and code from other sites (such as Unsplash, Wikimedia, cdnjs and jsDelivr), which receive your IP address when your browser fetches them. On the Find clubs page, maps and place searches come from OpenStreetMap; if you use your location or search for a place, it is used to show clubs near you.

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

10. Children

Fight Hub is for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we find that we have, we’ll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We’ll update this policy when the way we use information changes, and tell you about important changes in the app or by email. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

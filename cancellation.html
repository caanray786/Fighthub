Cancellations and refunds

How to cancel Premium, and how refunds work, including your 14 days to change your mind.

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Monthly Premium (£19.99 a month)

  • Cancel at any time in the app: open Premium, choose Manage subscription, then Cancel. You won’t be charged again, and Premium stays on until the end of the month you’ve already paid for.
  • We don’t refund part-months, except in your first 14 days (below) or where the law says otherwise.

Pause instead of cancelling

You can pause monthly Premium for 1 to 3 months instead of cancelling. You keep Premium until the end of the month you’ve already paid for, nothing is charged while you’re paused, and it restarts by itself. If you come back after your paid month has ended, a new month starts that day and is charged then. To pause, open Premium in the app, then Thinking of cancelling?

12 months of Premium (£203, paid once)

  • There is nothing to cancel: it doesn’t renew automatically.
  • We’ll remind you in the last 30 days. If you renew, the new 12 months are added to the end of your current ones.

Changed your mind? 14 days for a full refund

You can cancel within 14 days of starting a monthly subscription, or of buying or renewing a 12-month pass, and we’ll refund that payment in full. Your Premium ends when the refund is made. Later monthly renewals of the same subscription are not covered, but you can still cancel them at any time as described above.

How to ask: email support@fighthub.world from the email address on your account, saying you want to cancel and have a refund. You don’t have to give a reason. You can use the form below if you like, but you don’t have to.

We’ll refund you within 14 days of your request, to the card you paid with. There is no fee.

If something is wrong

If Premium or the app doesn’t work as described, tell us and we’ll put it right. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you’re entitled to have faulty digital content or services repaired or replaced, or, if we can’t fix them, some or all of your money back.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account cancels a monthly subscription straight away, and any Premium time left is lost. If you want a refund you’re entitled to, please email us before you delete your account, so we can find your payment quickly.

Price changes

If we change the monthly price, we’ll tell you at least 30 days before it applies to you, so you can cancel first if you prefer. A price change never affects a 12-month pass you’ve already paid for.

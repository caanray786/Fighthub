FightHub Images Directory
========================

You can place your custom local images here to replace the default Unsplash image presets.

Usage:
1. Copy your image files (JPG, PNG, WebP) into this directory.
2. In the Admin CRM panel, set the image field of a fighter, martial art, or news article to:
   images/<your-file-name> (e.g. images/ali.jpg)
3. Save the entry, and the site will dynamically display your custom image!
